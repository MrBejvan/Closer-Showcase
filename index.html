01 / The opportunity

Good intentions need a little support.

Remembering a friend's interview, birthday, or difficult week can be surprisingly hard when life gets busy. Closer began with the experience of managing social follow-through alongside ADHD-related attention and initiation challenges. The goal is not to automate friendship; it's to lower the effort of showing up.

PROBLEM

Important moments slip by

Calendar tools track dates, but don't always help turn those dates into meaningful outreach.

INTERVENTION

A gentle prompt

Surface an upcoming moment, then offer a short, editable message draft in an appropriate tone.

PRINCIPLE

People stay in control

Users choose what to share, what to say, and whether to send anything at all.

02 / Product & engineering approach

Designed for trust and low friction.

Interface choices

Muted sage and cream, clear language, readable spacing, and a combined home view of moments and connections. One obvious next action per event. No streaks, guilt, or urgency-driven engagement.

Message drafting

Editable warm, casual, and brief suggestions demonstrate the intended workflow. The current standalone prototype uses local templates, not live AI.

04 / Engineering maturity

What exists—and what comes next.

DEMONSTRATED

Interactive UX

Five connected screens, sample event creation, editable local message drafts, and responsive design.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Backend foundation

Authentication, social connections, event permissions, and database migrations have been drafted but not fully validated in a running environment.

NEXT GATE

Security verification

Clean build, multi-account authorization tests, account lifecycle controls, abuse prevention, and restricted staging before real-user testing.

The prototype is a concept demonstration, not a production-ready application. No claims of clinical effectiveness or validated accessibility compliance are made.
